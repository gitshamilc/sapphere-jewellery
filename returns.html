Return & Refund Policy

At SAPPHERE, we take immense pride in crafting bespoke, handcrafted royal jewelry. To maintain the highest quality and exclusivity, we adhere to the following return and exchange policies:

1. Returns & Exchanges Window

We accept return and exchange requests within 7 days of delivery. To be eligible, your items must be unused, in the same condition as received, and in their original premium packaging with hallmark tags intact.

2. Defective or Damaged Products

If you receive a product that is damaged or defective, we will offer a free replacement or complete refund. Please notify us on WhatsApp or Email within 24 hours of delivery with photographic/video proof of the damaged item.

3. Non-Returnable Items

The following items are not eligible for returns or refunds:

  • Customized jewelry or personalized engravings.
  • Items showing visible signs of wear, modification, or resizing by external jewelers.
  • Requests initiated after 7 days of delivery.

4. Return Shipping

To initiate a return, contact our support team. We will coordinate a secure, insured pick-up through our luxury shipping partners. Shipping costs for returning defective items are fully covered by SAPPHERE.

5. Refunds

Once your returned item is received and inspected at our atelier, we will notify you of status. Approved refunds will be processed via direct bank transfer within 3-5 business days.
